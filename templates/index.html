Sample Answers Pattern Scaling Notice

Extrapolation and Shuffle Confirmation

You entered a limited sample of answers (e.g. 5 total), but you requested 50 respondents.

How FormPilot will handle this:

  • The system will preserve your exact ratio distribution (e.g. 2 for 5, 3 for 4, 1 for 3) and extrapolate it across all respondents.
  • Only the rating values you specified will be used (unpicked ratings stay at 0).
  • Once generated, the system will completely shuffle the answers across all respondents so there is no repetitive or sequential pattern!
